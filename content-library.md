## TOPIC 1 — Yashica Y16: A $119 Camera That's Worse Than Your Phone (On Purpose)

### What Happened
On October 16, 2026, Yashica revealed full specs and pricing for the Y16, a tiny pocket digital camera styled after the 1959 Yashica Y16. It has a 12MP sensor, a 27mm-equivalent F12 lens, a built-in xenon flash, an optical viewfinder and a 1.45" screen. It weighs 93g, takes microSD, costs $119, and is open for pre-order with shipping expected late November. DPReview notes the image quality will be well below a typical smartphone.

### Why It Matters
It's part of the trend of people paying for low-fi, "imperfect" cameras. For photographers and creators it raises a real question: is the look you chase about sharpness, or about feel?

### Why It Suits Me
I'm a photographer and Pixel With Bliss owner. It's a gear story with a built-in debate (gimmick vs. creative tool), easy to talk about on camera, and I can show a real opinion on image quality versus character.

### Hook
This brand-new camera is $119, and even the reviewers say its photos will look worse than your phone. People are still going to buy it.

### Talking Points
- What it is: 12MP, tiny sensor, flash, optical viewfinder, 93g, $119, ships late November
- Reviewers say the quality is below a phone, and that is part of the appeal
- The "Yashica" name today is a licensed brand (Kyocera bought it in the 1980s, current cameras sold by 100 Enterprises), not the 1950s company

### My Angle
As a working photographer: gear doesn't make the shot, but the look people want is changing. Would I buy one as a cheap creative tool for content? Give an honest take, and say whether low-fi is a trend clients will ask for.

### 30–60 Second Script
