## TOPIC 1 — YouTube's New AI Editor Talks You Through Making Shorts

### What Happened
At its "Made on YouTube 2026" event on September 23, CEO Neal Mohan announced a new conversational AI editing tool built into YouTube Shorts and the YouTube Create app. Creators can type a prompt or pick a suggested one and the AI drafts a first cut, then keeps refining it as you chat — trimming pauses, reordering clips for pacing, and more. YouTube also rolled out AI-generated thumbnails matched to a channel's look and built-in A/B testing so creators can compare different cuts of the same video.

Editing is usually the biggest time sink for solo creators. If an AI can genuinely handle rough cuts and pacing fixes inside the app you're already shooting in, that changes how much content one person can put out in a week.
