Nikon says it is "carefully re-reviewing" the entry. The contest rules ban AI-generated video.

## TOPIC 2 — Lightroom Can Now Edit Your Photo With a Text Prompt

### What Happened
Adobe released Lightroom v9.6 on September 29, 2026, with an early-access feature called Prompt to Edit. You type what you want, like removing dust, colourising a photo or changing a sky, and Google's Nano Banana 2 model does it. It regenerates the whole image instead of moving sliders. It uses Adobe generative credits, runs on desktop only, and the edit stacks with your original.

### Why It Matters
This is a different kind of edit. Sliders adjust your pixels, but this regenerates the picture. That matters for client work, honesty and authenticity.

### Why It Suits Me
I edit photos and I'm learning AI tools. I can give a practical, honest take for working photographers.

### Hook
Lightroom now lets you edit a photo by typing a sentence, but there's a catch most people will miss.

### Talking Points
- Prompt to Edit runs on Google's Nano Banana 2 and is early access on desktop.
- It regenerates the entire photo and uses generative credits. It isn't a slider tweak.
- The edit stacks with your original, so you can compare and keep both.

### My Angle
Fine for restoring old family photos or fixing dust. I'd be careful using it on client or event work where the photo should be a true record. I'd test it on non-client images first and be open about it.

### 30–60 Second Script
Lightroom just added a way to edit photos by typing. It's called Prompt to Edit, and it came out in the 9.6 update on September 29. You write something like "remove the dust" or "make this black and white photo colour", and it does it. The catch is it doesn't move sliders. It regenerates the whole photo using Google's Nano Banana 2 model. It also uses Adobe's generative credits, and it's early access on desktop. The good part is your original stays stacked next to the new version. For old family photos, that's amazing. For client work, I'd be careful, because it's not really your original pixels anymore. I'm going to test it on my own photos first. Would you use it on client work?

## TOPIC 3 — Instagram's AI Assistant Now Reads Your Analytics

### What Happened
On September 30, 2026, Instagram started rolling out the Edits assistant to everyone using its Edits video app in the US. It looks at your views and retention, then suggests ideas and flags trending audio. It's free with a daily limit, and more usage will come with a Meta One subscription. Meta previewed it in June.

### Why It Matters
Creators can get content ideas from their own data without paying for a separate tool. It also shows how fast platforms are building AI into the creative process.

### Why It Suits Me
I make short-form video, edit in DaVinci Resolve, and I'm learning AI automation. I can compare it with my own workflow.

### Hook
Instagram's new AI assistant looks at your own stats and tells you what to post next.

### Talking Points
- It analyses your metrics and retention, brainstorms ideas and flags trending audio.
- Free with a daily limit in the Edits app in the US, with more usage via Meta One.
