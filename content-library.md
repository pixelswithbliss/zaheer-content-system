## TOPIC 1 — Canon Says Camera Prices Could Go Up Because of AI

### What Happened
Canon has said it may raise camera prices if memory chip costs stay high. Coverage from PetaPixel (Oct 30, 2026) and Digital Camera World reports a possible price increase in 2027. Fujifilm already raised list prices on several cameras effective Sept 1, 2026, and blamed the DRAM memory shortage. Canon has not raised prices yet.

### Why It Matters
Memory chips go into every camera and card. If costs keep rising, gear gets pricier, and buying now versus later becomes a real decision, especially for a creator on a budget.

### Why It Suits Me
It's camera gear and a creator-business topic, and it's relatable: I'm a pre-revenue photographer weighing gear purchases.

### Hook
AI might make your next camera more expensive, and Canon is already hinting at it.

### Talking Points
- Canon says price increases are possible if memory costs stay high. It hasn't confirmed one yet.
- Fujifilm already raised prices on several models, and said memory costs were the reason.
- Don't panic-buy. Buy gear that makes you money.

### My Angle
As a starting creator, I'd say don't panic-buy. Gear doesn't land clients, skills and a portfolio do. If you already need something for paid work, buying makes sense. If it's just gear envy, wait.
