## TOPIC 2 — ChatGPT Can Now Turn Your Audio Into Transcripts and Notes

### What Happened
According to OpenAI's release notes, on October 6 ChatGPT added audio file uploads. You can upload a recording and get a transcript, summary, structured notes or a follow-up draft, and ask questions about it. It's for paid plans and workspaces, and OpenAI warns transcripts can contain errors.

### Why It Matters
Talking-head creators speak their ideas out loud all the time. Turning a voice memo into a script outline or captions saves time, but errors mean you still need to check the output.

### Why It Suits Me
I record talking-head videos and I'm learning AI automation, so it fits my workflow of voice note to script to captions.

### Hook

### Talking Points

- OpenAI itself says transcripts can have mistakes, so always check.
- Use case: record a rambling voice note, get a clean outline.
