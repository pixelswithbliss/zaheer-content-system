## TOPIC 1 — DaVinci Resolve 21.1 Adds Built-In AI Assistant Integration

### What Happened
Blackmagic Design released DaVinci Resolve 21.1 on September 8, 2026, adding over 100 new tools and controls. The headline feature is AI assistant integration — editors can now connect Resolve to AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT for project management and media review tasks. The update also upgrades the Photo page (improved RAW post-decode sharpening, DNG lens distortion/aberration correction, Sky Replacement for stills) and adds support for new camera log formats: DJI D-Gamut2/D-Log2, GoPro Log, and Leica L-Log.

### Why It Matters
Editing software has been racing to bake in AI, but most of it lives in third-party plugins. Resolve — one of the most widely used NLEs, with a free tier — building AI assistant hooks directly into the core app signals where mainstream editing workflows are heading. The log format and Photo page updates are also immediately practical for anyone mixing footage from multiple camera brands.

### Why It Suits Me
I edit every project in DaVinci Resolve, so this update is about my own daily toolkit — I can demo it live on real footage instead of just reporting on it secondhand.

### Hook

### Talking Points

- Photo page gets real upgrades: RAW sharpening, DNG lens correction, Sky Replacement for stills
- New camera log format support (DJI, GoPro, Leica) matters for anyone mixing camera brands on a shoot

## TOPIC 2 — OM System PEN Officially Launches at $999

### What Happened
On September 9, 2026, OM System officially launched the OM SYSTEM PEN — the first PEN-series camera under the OM System brand. It's a retro, rangefinder-style Micro Four Thirds mirrorless camera with a built-in 2.36M-dot OLED electronic viewfinder, a 20.4MP Four Thirds sensor, in-body stabilization rated up to 5.5 stops, a 3.2-inch flip-out touchscreen, and IPX1 weather sealing (with a compatible lens). It ships late October 2026 at $999.99 body-only, or $1,099.99 with the 14-42mm kit lens.

### Why It Matters
A weather-sealed mirrorless camera with a built-in EVF for under $1,000 is a rare combination at this price point, and it revives a compact camera line — the PEN — that photographers have wanted back for years.

### Why It Suits Me
Gear talk consistently resonates with my audience, and as someone shooting events and cinematic work, a compact, weather-sealed body like this is a realistic conversation about a lightweight backup or travel camera.
