## TOPIC 1 — Instagram Is Testing 90-Minute Videos ("Features")

### What Happened
On October 26, 2026, Instagram announced a test of "Features," an upload type for horizontal videos up to 90 minutes long. Creators who use it also get "Clips as Reels," which turns segments of the long video into Reels that link back to the full video, and viewers can send the long video to the Instagram app on their TV. It has been tested with creators in Brazil and is expanding on a small scale in the US (Tubefilter reports tens of thousands of US creators over the coming months). Meta says it's too early to share a long-term way for creators to earn from it.

### Why It Matters
Instagram has pushed short vertical video for years and shut down IGTV, its last long-form try. Now it's going after YouTube's TV watch time. For creators, it could mean a new place to repurpose long videos, but there is no direct payment attached yet, so it's a test, not a sure thing.

### Why It Suits Me
I'm a Toronto content creator and photographer who edits in DaVinci Resolve and already makes short talking-head videos. A long-form-to-Reels pipeline is directly relevant to how I plan and repurpose content, and it affects the Instagram portfolio side of Pixel With Bliss.

### Hook
Instagram just brought back long videos, up to 90 minutes, and this time they're coming for YouTube.

### Talking Points
- Instagram is testing "Features": horizontal videos up to 90 minutes, plus "Clips as Reels" that link back to the full video.
- It's only a test (Brazil, small US rollout), and there's no payment model yet. Meta says it's exploring ways for creators to earn from brands, fans, and Instagram.
- Instagram tried this before with IGTV and it flopped, so the question is whether it works this time.

### My Angle
As a small creator with no big audience yet, I'm not rushing to rebuild around this. But the smart move is making one long video (a full event recap or a behind-the-scenes edit), then cutting it into Reels. That works on YouTube today and could work on Instagram if this sticks. Don't chase a feature that doesn't pay yet. Get ready for it.

### 30–60 Second Script
Instagram just announced something big, and honestly it feels like déjà vu. They're testing a new thing called Features, which lets you upload horizontal videos up to 90 minutes long. Ninety minutes. On Instagram.

It also comes with something called Clips as Reels. So you cut short pieces out of your long video, post them as Reels, and each one links back to the full video. They're even pushing it toward TV, so people can watch on the big screen. This is Instagram going straight after YouTube.

Now, a few things to keep in mind. It's a test right now. It started with creators in Brazil, and it's rolling out to a small group in the US. And there's no way to get paid for it yet. Meta just said they're exploring it.

Also, remember IGTV? Instagram tried long video before, and it didn't go anywhere.

So here's my take. Don't rebuild your whole strategy around it. But if you shoot events or make longer edits, start thinking in one long video, then lots of short clips. That works everywhere, and if Instagram's version takes off, you're already ready.
