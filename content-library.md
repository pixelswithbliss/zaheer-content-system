## TOPIC 1 — A Photographer Just Got Called Out for AI Images in a Portfolio

### What Happened
PetaPixel reported on Nov 3, 2026 that a photographer, Dave Wilson, ran another photographer's portfolio through OpenAI's Verify tool. It checks for C2PA metadata or SynthID watermarks. He says nearly the whole portfolio showed SynthID marks. The studio, Aerial Perspectives, blamed a former team member for one image and denied the whole portfolio is AI-generated. This is an allegation, not a proven finding.

### Why It Matters
Clients and other photographers can now check images for hidden AI watermarks. Trust in portfolios is becoming a real issue. Detection has limits: OpenAI says Verify only covers OpenAI-made images, and a negative result isn't proof an image is real.
