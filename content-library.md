## TOPIC 1 — Facebook Is Testing a Reels-First App

### What Happened
Meta is testing a version of Facebook that opens straight into full-screen Reels instead of the usual feed. The test is running in video-heavy markets like India and Indonesia, users can opt out, and the classic feed stays as a second tab. Meta announced this direction in July; TechCrunch reported the test on Oct 6. Meta says it will explore bringing it to the US in 2027.

### Why It Matters
If Facebook becomes video-first, Reels become the front door of the app. Short-form creators get more shots at being seen, and photo or text posts may get less attention.

### Why It Suits Me
I post Reels on Facebook already, and I run Pixel With Bliss. How Facebook treats video affects both my reach and my future clients' content.

### Hook
Facebook might soon open straight to Reels, not your feed. If you make video, pay attention.

### Talking Points
- Meta is testing a Reels-first Facebook in India and Indonesia, with an opt-out.
- Meta says it may explore the US in 2027, so this is a test, not a rollout.
- Video-first platforms reward consistent short-form creators.

### My Angle
Don't panic-pivot. Photographers should use video to show their photo work (behind-the-scenes, before/after) so the portfolio still gets seen in a video-first world. It's a test; plan, don't overreact.

### 30–60 Second Script
Facebook might soon open straight to Reels instead of your feed. Meta is testing this right now in India and Indonesia. When you open the app, you land on full-screen video, and the normal feed is just a second tab. People can opt out, and Meta says it may explore the US in 2027, so this is a test, not a rollout. But the direction is clear: video first. As a photographer, that matters to me. If your content is just still photos, it could get less attention. So I'm putting my work into short video: behind the scenes, before and afters, quick tips. Same photos, just shown in a way the platform actually wants. Are you making Reels for Facebook, or ignoring it? Tell me below.

## TOPIC 2 — AI Agents Are Getting Blocked by Websites

### What Happened
Personal AI agents like Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots, which shop and book things for you, are running into blocks from major sites. TechCrunch (Oct 6) reports Amazon explicitly blocked Muse, and others like Walmart and airlines have checkpoints or security concerns that stop agents. Meta, Walmart, Stripe and partners are reportedly working on an open standard so legitimate agents can be told apart from bots.

### Why It Matters
Anyone building automations is affected. If sites don't let agents in, scraping-style automations break, and official APIs and integrations become more important.

### Why It Suits Me
I'm learning AI automation (n8n and similar). This is a real-world lesson about what automations can and can't reliably do.

### Hook
Everyone says AI agents will do everything for you. Websites are already shutting the door on them.

### Talking Points
- Amazon blocked Meta's Muse agent (reported Sept 21-22; TechCrunch's Oct 6 piece covers the wider problem).
- Other sites block agents too, some on purpose, some by accident through human-verification checks.
- Meta, Walmart, Stripe and partners are reportedly working on an open standard.

### My Angle
As someone learning automation: build on official APIs and connectors, not on tricking websites. It's more reliable and won't break the moment a site changes.

### 30–60 Second Script
Everyone says AI agents will do your shopping and bookings for you. But websites are already blocking them. TechCrunch reports Amazon blocked Meta's new Muse agent, and other big sites have security checks that stop agents too. Meta, Walmart, Stripe and others are reportedly working on a shared standard so real agents can be told apart from bots. I'm learning automation, and this is a good lesson. If your workflow depends on a bot pretending to be a human on a website, it can break overnight. Using official APIs and proper connectors is slower to set up, but it lasts. So when I build my own automations, I start with the official route first. Have you had an automation break on you?
