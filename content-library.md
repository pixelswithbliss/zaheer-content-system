## TOPIC 1 — Adobe Puts Photoshop, Lightroom & Firefly Tools Inside Google Gemini (and Acrobat Inside Claude)

### What Happened
Adobe announced that Photoshop, Lightroom, Adobe Express, and Firefly tools are now built directly into Google Gemini, so anyone can type a plain-language request — like "match the color and lighting across this whole shoot" — and Gemini will carry it out using real Adobe tools behind the scenes, no separate app needed. In the same announcement, Adobe brought Acrobat into Anthropic's Claude. This follows Adobe's earlier integrations with ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and Slack — Adobe is betting people will increasingly start creative work inside an AI chat assistant instead of opening Adobe's own software first.

## TOPIC 2 — Adobe Completes $340M Acquisition of Topaz Labs

### What Happened
Adobe officially closed its acquisition of Topaz Labs — makers of Gigapixel AI, DeNoise, Photo AI, and Video AI — on September 23, 2026, in a deal reported at roughly $340 million (originally agreed June 25, 2026). Adobe confirmed Topaz will remain a standalone brand and app at current pricing for now, while its AI models get folded into Creative Cloud tools like Photoshop over time. Topaz Labs CEO Eric Yang is joining Adobe's Digital Video and Audio team.

Topaz's tools are among the most widely used third-party AI upscaling and denoising tools in photo and video editing. Adobe acquiring them signals further consolidation of AI editing technology under one company — which affects pricing, competition, and where innovation happens next.
