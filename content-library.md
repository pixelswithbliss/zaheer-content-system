# 2026-10-14

## TOPIC 1 — Sigma's $799 APS-C "70-200" Is Coming

### What Happened
On October 8, 2026, Sigma announced the 50-120mm F2.8 DC OS | Contemporary, a telephoto zoom made for APS-C cameras. It keeps f/2.8 the whole way through, covers about 75-180mm in full-frame terms, and is available for Sony E, Fujifilm X and Canon RF. It goes on sale October 22 at $799 (US, per Fstoppers; Sigma's page doesn't list a price). Stabilization is rated at 6.5 stops at 50mm and 4.5 stops at 120mm.

### Why It Matters
Fast telephoto zooms for APS-C are rare, and full-frame 70-200mm f/2.8 lenses cost a lot more and weigh much more. This lens is only 390g (Sony/Fuji versions), which is easy to carry through a long event shoot. It also has built-in stabilization, which helps video shooters and cameras without in-body stabilization.

### Why It Suits Me
I cover events and shoot photo and video. A small, cheaper f/2.8 telephoto is exactly the gear a solo operator with no client budget yet would consider. It is a gear-value story, not a spec dump.

### Hook
This lens might be the cheapest way to get a 70-200 f/2.8 look on a crop-sensor camera.

### Talking Points
- Sigma announced the 50-120mm f/2.8 for APS-C: about 75-180mm equivalent, sale date October 22, $799.
- It weighs only about 390g and has stabilization, so it works for handheld event and video work.
- The catch: f/2.8 on APS-C gives about the depth of field of f/4 on full frame, so you get less background blur than a full-frame 70-200 f/2.8. No Nikon Z or L-mount version. Only manufacturer claims so far, no independent tests.

### My Angle
Frame it as a starter-photographer question: do you need full frame to cover events, or is a lighter, cheaper APS-C kit enough? Be honest that I haven't tested it, and say I'm waiting for real reviews before recommending anyone buy it.
