### Topic
VSCO now says it won't train generative AI on ANY user photos (free or paid)

### Normalized Topic
VSCO updated its Creator Content Standards (effective Nov 10, 2026) to state that public or private creator content from both paid and free users will not be used to train generative AI; previously free users' public content could be used. Partners must also contractually agree not to train generative AI on public content. Non-generative AI uses (recommendations, moderation) can still use public content.
